Task Suite Design

HUMANO organizes 100 tasks into eight complementary suites that evaluate fundamental whole-body skills, goal-directed loco-manipulation, generalization, temporal skill composition, and state-dependent decision making.

02

Generalization Settings

Beyond the variations built into the task suites, HUMANO-Plus evaluates generalization under seven controlled distribution shifts spanning distractors, lighting, backgrounds, camera viewpoints, language, sensor noise, and the robot’s initial state.

Demonstration Collection
and Rendering
